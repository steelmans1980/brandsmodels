# BMW X5 (bmw-x5) — research notes

## Coverage
E53 (1999–2006, 2003 facelift), E70 (2006–2013, 2010 LCI; X5 M), F15 (2013–2018; F85 X5 M, xDrive40e), G05 (2018–2026; G18 LWB China, F95 X5 M, xDrive45e, 2023 LCI with xDrive50e), and G65 (fifth generation, revealed 30 June 2026, production from August 2026).

G65 was not in the assignment list but BMW PressClub (global and US releases of 30.06.2026) and Wikipedia document it, and Wikipedia gives G05 production as "August 2018 – 2026". So G05 is recorded as ended (2026, year only) and G65 as ongoing. Reviewer: confirm this is wanted.

## Conflicts / caveats
- E53 assembly: Wikipedia lists Toluca, Mexico; BMW (2006) says built exclusively in Spartanburg. Only Spartanburg recorded.
- E53/E70 model years: Wikipedia infoboxes give 2000–2007 and 2007–2014, overlapping the next generation. E53 model years omitted; E70 only "from 2007".
- E70 wheelbase gain: BMW UK +113 mm; Wikipedia +110 mm. Manufacturer recorded.
- E70 X5 M debut: Wikipedia says New York April 2009; a BMW US pricing release (seen in search results only) reportedly says world debut in Shanghai. Not recorded as a date.
- G05 LCI production: BMW said (Feb 2023) it would start April 2023; Wikipedia says March 2023.
- UK/EU "hp" = PS (e.g. G05 LCI xDrive50e 490 hp UK incl. boost vs 483 hp US). Records kept per market.
- Wikipedia E53/E70/F15 engine tables: "Years" column not explicitly calendar vs model year.
- check-data's number normaliser merges adjacent numbers ("4,200 576" → 4200576), so some quotes are split into two parts.
