## Structure
- One line, four generations: W163, W164, W166 (M-Class, then GLE from the 2015 facelift) and V167 (GLE).
- W166: revision r1 has kind "rename". It covers the April 2015 New York facelift and the rename to GLE. The generation lists both names, M-Class and GLE.
- Coupés are body styles. C292 is b2 of W166 and C167 is b2 of V167. The China-only long-wheelbase GLE L (from 2026) is b3 of V167.
- V167 has two facelifts: 2023 (2024 model year) and March 2026 (2027 model year, production from August 2026, per English Wikipedia).

## Assembly and US model years
- All generations are built at MBUSI in Vance (Tuscaloosa County), Alabama.
- W163 was also built by Magna Steyr in Graz (1999–2002).
- Later generations were also assembled from kits in India and Indonesia (W166) and at more sites for V167.
- North American model years: 1998–2005, 2006–2011, 2012–2019 and 2020 onward.

## Conflicts and points to double-check
- V167 code: English Wikipedia says W167, German Wikipedia says V 167. V 167 is recorded. Commons uses "Mercedes-Benz W167".
- W163 production: English Wikipedia gives Feb 1997 – Dec 2004, German Wikipedia gives 1997–2005.
- W164 production start: English Wikipedia gives Jan 2005, German Wikipedia gives 03/2005. For the end, German Wikipedia gives 06/2011 in the infobox and July 2011 in the text. The two Wikipedias' dimensions also differ by 1–2 mm.
- W164 facelift reveal: English Wikipedia says New York in March 2008, German Wikipedia says late April 2008.
- W166 production end: English Wikipedia gives 2019, German Wikipedia gives 2018.
- W166 reveal: German Wikipedia gives IAA 2011 (recorded). English Wikipedia mentions a pre-production introduction on 10 June 2011.
- English Wikipedia claims Stuttgart production for Europe and Australia (2011–2015). This contradicts German Wikipedia and is not recorded.
- Engine tables from English Wikipedia carry no market, so they are recorded as "unstated". US figures from MBUSA are in hp and lb-ft.
