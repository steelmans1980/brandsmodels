# Volkswagen Tiguan (volkswagen-tiguan)

## Sources consulted
- Volkswagen Newsroom (volkswagen-newsroom.com), which is readable for HTML press releases. Releases used: 5963 (six million), 6167 (2020 facelift), 6638 (Tiguan R), 7146, 7170 and 7597 (Allspace update), 17326 (final set-up drives), 17655 (third-generation premiere), 17886 (European pre-sales), 18182 (eHybrid), 19001 (2.0 TSI 4MOTION) and 20508 (EDITION 20 history).
- Press kits are PDF downloads, which text.mjs cannot read, so no full technical-data sheet was used.
- The 2015 IAA premiere release (1786) returns 404.
- media.vw.com (US) renders only with JavaScript, so no US manufacturer source is available.
- English Wikipedia "Volkswagen Tiguan" (oldid 1376988162). German Wikipedia: VW Tiguan I, II and III. "VW Tiguan Allspace" is a redirect, and there is no article for the North American third generation.

## Market distinctions
- Generation 1 (5N): the US sold it again as the "Tiguan Limited" for 2017–2018. China built a long-wheelbase version (2,684 mm) and kept selling the "Tiguan Silk Road" until 2020.
- Generation 2 (AD1; long wheelbase BW2/BJ2): the normal wheelbase is sold as "Tiguan" in Europe and elsewhere.
  - Europe: the long wheelbase is the "Tiguan Allspace".
  - North America: only the long wheelbase is sold, simply as "Tiguan".
  - China: the long wheelbase is the "Tiguan L", plus the Tiguan X coupé.
  - The 2020 facelift (normal wheelbase) and the 2021 Allspace update are separate revisions.
- Generation 3 (CT1; German Wikipedia "Typ CT"): the North American 2025 Tiguan is a long-wheelbase car that Wikipedia describes as nearly identical to the Chinese Tayron L. It is kept in this generation as body style b2, with its own US dimension record. China sells the long-wheelbase Tiguan L Pro.
  - North American model years and a separate code for the North American version were not documented in a readable source.

## Conflicts and points to double-check
- Gen-3 dimensions: English Wikipedia gives 4,551/1,842/1,640/2,681 mm. German Wikipedia gives 4,539 mm length and 2,677–2,680 mm wheelbase. The German length is recorded; it needs checking against the VW press kit.
- Gen-1 sales start: VW says the market launch was in 2007 (recorded). English Wikipedia says early 2008 in Europe; German Wikipedia says August 2007.
- Gen-2 sales start: VW says April 2016 (recorded). German Wikipedia says January 2016.
- Gen-2 long-wheelbase extension: VW says +110 mm; Wikipedia gives +109 mm.
- Gen-3 production: VW planned Wolfsburg production from fall 2023; Wikipedia says 2024. German Wikipedia gives sales from November 2023 and deliveries from February 2024.

## Gaps
- No full manufacturer dimensions for any generation; the press kits are PDFs.
- No numeric seating source (5/7).
- The gen-1 length is only a range (4,426–4,457 mm, German Wikipedia), so it is not recorded.
- Some VW powertrain entries lack torque.
- The gen-2 European eHybrid output comes from German Wikipedia (180 kW), not from VW.
