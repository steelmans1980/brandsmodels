# Audi Q7 (audi-q7): research notes

Generations: 4L (2005–2015, 2009 facelift), 4M (2015–2026, 2019 and 2024 facelifts), G8 (revealed June 2026). I added the third generation (G8) because Audi announced it in June 2026 and both Audi and Wikipedia document it.

## Sources used
- English Wikipedia "Audi Q7", oldid 1376476779: codes, dates, model years (EU and NA for the 4L), assembly, dimensions, engine tables.
- German Wikipedia "Audi Q7 4L" (oldid 263191743) and "Audi Q7 4M" (oldid 268908440): facelift timing, 4.2 TDI update, 150 kW TDI, weight claim, aluminium panels, all-wheel steering, e-tron data, cargo.
- Audi MediaCenter (manufacturer):
  - "Strong in design, comfort, and technology: The revised Audi Q7" (30 Jan 2024): the 2024 facelift, 2024 engines, a first-generation retrospective, and 5-seat cargo.
  - "Maximum efficiency in ample space: the new Audi Q7 TFSI e quattro…" (23 Apr 2024): 2024 PHEV figures and the 25.9 kWh battery.
  - "Third generation of the successful SUV: new Audi Q7…" (9 Jun 2026): G8 engines, cargo, quattro, Bratislava, ordering and deliveries.

## Sources that failed
- Old Audi MediaCenter press releases (for example the 2015 Detroit world premiere, id 1369) return 404 since the site moved to audi.com. The new MediaCenter's search lists only the three Q7 releases above.
- Audi technical data sheets (uploads.audi.com eTD-*.pdf) are PDFs and text.mjs cannot read them.
- media.audiusa.com is JS-only (no text).
- I did not read the German Wikipedia article for the G8; the permalink lookup timed out under Wikimedia rate limiting.

## Gaps and conflicts (please double-check)
- 4L production start: Wikipedia says November 2005, while Audi 2024 says "start of production in 2006". I used Wikipedia's date.
- 4L facelift: German Wikipedia says spring 2009 and Pebble Beach 2009, but the English Wikipedia section heading reads "Facelift (2010–2015)".
- 4M 3.0 TDI e-tron system output: German Wikipedia gives 275 kW / 700 Nm (recorded). English Wikipedia's table gives 285 kW (387 PS) and its prose gives 190 kW / 600 Nm.
- SQ7 TFSI displacement: English Wikipedia says 3,956 cc and German Wikipedia says 3,996 cm³. I left it out.
- 4M model years 2016–2026 have no market in the source. North American model years for the 4M were not confirmed separately.
- I have no manufacturer dimensions for any generation. The G8 dimensions are from English Wikipedia only (2,010 mm / 1,800 mm look rounded) and are provisional.
- G8 production start is undocumented ("2026 (to commence)"). The G8 2.9 V6 petrol and SQ7 V8 figures come from English Wikipedia, which cites US press; market is "unstated".
- Seating (5/6/7) is described only in words, so it is not stored as structured seating data. For the G8 it is recorded as a change item instead.
- Most engine figures have no market in the sources and are recorded as "unstated".
