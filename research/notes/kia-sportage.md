# Kia Sportage: research notes

Family file: `data/families/kia-sportage.json`. Generations:
- NB (1993; incl. 3-door soft-top and the extended "Sportage Grand"/"Grand Wagon")
- JE/KM (2004; two facelifts)
- SL (2010; Sportage R; 2014MY facelift)
- QL (2015; 2018 facelift, plus the China-only KX5 facelift)
- NQ5 (2021; SWB/LWB, HEV/PHEV, plus the 2024 facelift)

## Sources used
- English Wikipedia, "Kia Sportage", oldid 1378656280 (secondary).
- Kia Heritage Archive on worldwide.kia.com (manufacturer):
  - Sportage (1993), with length/width/height and displacements
  - Sportage R (2010)
  - The All-New Sportage (2021)
  - History chapter 2: the Sportage was unveiled at the Tokyo Motor Show in October 1991
- "Shaping the Sportage" story on worldwide.kia.com (manufacturer). Covers each generation's design, and states that Europe and South America got the SWB car while Korea and North America got the LWB.
- Kia Europe press, 2018 Sportage upgrade (July 2018).
- Kia Europe press kit, All-new Kia Sportage (6 Dec 2021). Gives the EU SWB dimensions, 591 L VDA luggage, and the powertrains.
- Kia Austria press kit "Pressemappe Kia Sportage" (German). Gives the predecessor's luggage (max. 503 L) and EU luggage by powertrain.

## Market distinctions
- NB: Karmann (Osnabrück) built the European cars from 1995 to 1998. Diesel and SOHC petrol versions were mostly sold in Europe. In China it was the "Wanli Sportage".
- SL: sold as the "Sportage R" (in China, alongside the KM).
- QL: sold in China as the "Kia KX5", which got its own March 2019 facelift.
- NQ5 comes in two bodies:
  - EU SWB: 4,515 / 1,865 / 1,645 / 2,680 mm (Kia Europe).
  - LWB: 4,660 mm long on a 2,755 mm wheelbase (Wikipedia). Kia's heritage page gives 4,685 mm without naming the body.
  - Brazil and South Africa also get the SWB (Wikipedia; mentioned in notes only).
  - In Pakistan the LWB car is the "Sportage L" (no market code, so not recorded).

## Conflicts and caveats
- NB width: Wikipedia gives 1,855 mm and Kia's heritage page gives 1,735 mm. The Kia figure is recorded. Wikipedia's two wheelbases are not tied to body styles, so they are not recorded.
- KM: the code is "JE/KM" per Wikipedia (the slug uses km). The V6 is 173 PS in the prose and 175 HP in the table.
- Wikipedia's KM/SL engine tables use "HP" without saying metric or SAE, so they are recorded as hp.
- QL length: Wikipedia gives 4,480 mm, while Kia Austria gives the predecessor as 4.485 mm (written with dot separators, so only noted).
- NQ5 EU height: Kia Europe gives 1,645 mm and Kia Austria gives 1,650 mm.

## Gaps
- No manufacturer specification source for KM. There are no US manufacturer figures for any generation, because kianewscenter could not be read.
- There are no seating sources.
- Facelift dates:
  - KM facelifts (May 2008; UK early 2009) and the KX5 facelift are recorded as salesStart only.
  - The SL facelift is dated only by model year (2014).
  - The NQ5 facelift has a revealed year of 2024 (Kia story) and Wikipedia's "released 5 November 2024" with no market.
- NB facelifts (1998, 2002) are only mentioned in photo captions, so they are not recorded.
- No quotable outputs for the NB 2.2 diesel.
- The NB four-wheel drive type (part-time or not) is not stated, so it is recorded as AWD with a note.
