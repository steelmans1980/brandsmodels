# Porsche Cayenne (porsche-cayenne): research notes

Generations:
- 9PA / E1 (2002–2009), with the Type 957 model update as a revision
- 92A / E2 (2010–2017), with the 2014 facelift
- E3 (9YA/9YB, body codes 9Y0/9Y3, "PO536"; 2017–), with the Cayenne Coupé body style and the 2023 facelift
- E4 Cayenne Electric (X1A/X1B; revealed Nov 2025, production Feb 2026)

## Decisions
- **957 = revision, not a generation.** Porsche calls it "the model update of the first-generation Cayenne" / "model update of the E1 generation" / "E1 II". English Wikipedia says "facelifted 9PA chassis Cayenne (957)" and German Wikipedia says "Modellpflege" of Typ 9PA.
- **Electric Cayenne = fourth generation.** Porsche (2022) says "The fourth generation of the Porsche Cayenne will be all-electric", and English/German Wikipedia say "fourth generation (Type X1A/X1B or E4)" / "Typ E4". It is sold alongside the E3 combustion and PHEV models: Porsche says it "complements the existing offering…" and German Wikipedia says "parallel zum PO536".
- **Slugs.** I used 9pa and 92a (Wikipedia model codes). I used e3 and e4 (Porsche's own designations) because the third generation's codes conflict: 9YA/9YB (en WP), 9Y0/9Y3 body codes (en WP), PO536 (de WP). All of these codes are recorded.
- **Markets.** Porsche Newsroom's terms say its vehicle data "refers to the German market", so Porsche-sourced figures are marked EU.

## Not usable
- Porsche PDF press kits (2017, 2023, 2019) cannot be read by text.mjs. That is where the E3 dimensions and the SUV cargo figure (770 L) would have come from.
- The HTML press kit pages for the 2023 facelift give no exterior dimensions.

## Gaps and conflicts (please double-check)
- E1 GTS power: Porsche gives 298 kW (405 PS) (recorded); the unsourced Wikipedia table gives 411 PS.
- 2017 Cayenne S torque: Porsche 550 Nm vs Wikipedia 549 Nm. 2023 Cayenne S: Porsche 349 kW (474 PS) vs Wikipedia 475 PS.
- E4 wheelbase: Porsche 3,023 mm vs Wikipedia 3,024 mm.
- The English Wikipedia engine tables for E1 and E2 are tagged "does not cite any sources"; they are used only where Porsche gives no figure.
- Cayenne Coupé reveal: Porsche presented it on 21 Mar 2019 and showed it at Auto Shanghai in April 2019 as its "Asian premiere". English Wikipedia says it was revealed at Shanghai.
- E3 dimensions: Wikipedia gives only ranges, so only the 2,895 mm wheelbase is recorded.
- "958" (a common enthusiast code for the E2) appears in no source read, so it is not recorded.
- English Wikipedia's E2 infobox lists a coupe body style, which appears erroneous; not recorded.
- Model years (2003–2010, 2011–2018, 2019–) are given without a market.
- English Wikipedia's "sales beginning in 2019" for the E3 is suspicious; not recorded.
