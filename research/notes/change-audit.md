The most common problems were:
- market tags such as "(US)", "Europe:" or "Japanese figure" that the quote does not state
- engine codes, names, sizes or outputs added from memory (N62, PR25DD, 19.7 kWh, T8 Twin Engine, Thor's Hammer)
- claims about what was dropped or replaced that the quote does not make
