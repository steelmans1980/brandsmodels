# Toyota RAV4 (toyota-rav4) — research notes

Generations: XA10 (1994), XA20 (2000), XA30 (2005), XA40 (2012), XA50 (2018), XA60 (2025). One line ("rav4"); the
Japanese long-wheelbase XA30 sold as Vanguard is recorded as a market name plus a separate body style, not a separate line.

## Sources used
- Wikipedia en `Toyota_RAV4` oldid 1378807043; de `Toyota_RAV4` oldid 271178747.
- Toyota Motor Corporation (global.toyota): 1994 launch (detail/7862794), 2000 XA20 Japan launch (detail/7932597),
  2005 XA30 Japan launch with dimension table (detail/246605), 2007 Vanguard launch (detail/279304), 2019 XA50 Japan
  launch (newsroom 27541875), 2025 XA60 world premiere (newsroom 42758168).
- Toyota (GB) media site: "20 Years of the Toyota RAV4" (generation timeline with length deltas), archive pages for the
  1995 five-door and 1998 revision, RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid release.
- Toyota USA Newsroom (pressroom.toyota.com): 2013 LA debut, 2016 RAV4 Hybrid/facelift NYIAS, 2019 world debut notice
  and full launch release, RAV4 Prime LA 2019, 2026 RAV4 releases.

## Failed / not usable
- pressroom.toyota.com "product specifications" posts (2010, 2012, 2013, 2016, 2019) are only PDF downloads — not
  readable by text.mjs. Same for media.toyota.co.uk spec PDFs. pressroom WP REST API requires auth (post sitemap used to
  discover URLs instead).
- newsroom.toyota.eu search returned no usable RAV4 pages; global.toyota gallery page is JS-only.
- No readable source found for XA30 long-wheelbase (US/AU) dimensions, or full XA60 dimensions (only ranges).

## Conflicts / reviewer checks
- XA20 length increase: Toyota Japan +45/+30 mm vs Toyota GB +55/+40 mm (3-door/5-door).
- XA40 length increase: Toyota GB +235 mm vs de Wikipedia +205 mm.
- XA10 launch height 1,695 mm (Toyota JP release) vs 1,646–1,661 mm (Wikipedia).
- XA50 US petrol output 203 hp (Toyota USA) vs 151 kW/202 hp (Wikipedia).
- XA60 US PHEV 320 hp (May 2025 debut) vs 324 hp (later 2026 release); recorded 324.
- XA30 V6 200 kW (Wikipedia table) vs 201 kW (Wikipedia prose).
- Revision dates for XA10/XA20/XA30 facelifts are European/UK sales dates from de Wikipedia/Toyota GB; North
  American facelifts were for MY1998, MY2004, MY2009 and MY2011 respectively.
- Several Wikipedia engine-table rows are European specs but carry no market in the source → "unstated".
- Checker quirk (fixed): numbers written like "73.0"/"67.0" previously could not be recorded as 73/67; the XA50 LE/XLE width and height are now recorded on d1 (spec-audit.md).

## Moved from public notes
- XA30: no sourced long-wheelbase dimensions were found in a readable source (Toyota USA spec sheets are PDF downloads).
- XA50: US LE/XLE width/height (73.0 in, 67.0 in) were previously not recorded because the checker required the number exactly as written; now recorded on d1.
